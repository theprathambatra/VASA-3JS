The approximate model uses a body of `1.33 × 1.78 × 1.07` arbitrary world units. The photographs do not establish an exact physical depth or production dimensions. The sprayer is concealed in the references; its shape is an inferred interaction detail. The wordmark and floral device are carefully drawn approximations rather than supplied vector artwork.

The scene was visually checked from front, side, rear, desktop, and mobile views. The cap lift and turntable controls were also checked in a browser. The original img2threejs repository's full automated pass and scoring pipeline was not run end to end; this deliverable applies its component inventory and procedural construction method to a manually refined model.
